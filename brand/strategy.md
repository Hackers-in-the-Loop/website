# Hackers in the Loop — Brand Strategy

## Purpose

Keep the machinery of agentic computing open, inspectable, composable, and in human hands.

Hackers in the Loop builds the shared technical substrate beneath agentic applications: runtimes, DSLs, execution models, credentials, isolation, WASM components, kernels, hardware experiments, and the tools that connect them. It is also a place where technically serious people help one another learn, test ideas, publish work, and turn experiments into durable open-source infrastructure.

## Positioning

**Hackers in the Loop is an open-source hackerspace for the systems beneath agentic software.**

It is not:

- an AI product company;
- a generic meetup or “innovation lab”;
- a framework-specific user group;
- a futuristic AGI brand built around magic, brains, or autonomous robots.

It is the place for people who want to understand, modify, and improve how agentic systems actually work.

## Descriptor

**Open source for agentic systems.**

This is the plain-language category statement. It appears in the primary badge and should accompany the name wherever a new audience needs immediate context.

## Brand promise

**Keep intelligence hackable.**

“Hackable” carries the proposition: open to inspection, modification, composition, experimentation, and contribution. It preserves the constructive hacker ethic of learning how systems work and using that understanding to improve them.

## Community character

- **Technically serious:** Work earns respect through depth, rigor, and what gets built.
- **Open by construction:** Code, mechanisms, decisions, and learning happen in public.
- **Hands-on:** Contributors learn by making, testing, breaking, repairing, and shipping.
- **Human-directed:** Agents amplify human agency; they do not obscure responsibility.
- **Composable:** Small primitives and clear interfaces are more valuable than sealed platforms.
- **Generous:** Members help each other cross disciplines, from language design to GPU kernels.
- **Playful, not frivolous:** Hacker culture supplies wit and curiosity without sacrificing quality.

## Central brand idea: the workshop stamp

The identity is a compact typographic mark that can live on a repository, a community avatar, an event poster, or the side of a physical machine.

- **HACK** states the posture: inspect, modify, experiment, and build.
- **HITL** is both the organization’s short name and a reference to human-in-the-loop systems.
- **The stack** connects software stacks, call stacks, layered infrastructure, and people building on one another’s work.
- **The signal line** is a visible path ending at a circular human-intervention node. It represents action, contribution, and agency.
- **The equipment-plate shape** makes the community feel like a working hackerspace rather than a polished AI vendor.

The mark is intentionally typographic. Its meaning is carried by the name, the double stack, and a single repeatable signal—not by a generic brain, robot, neural web, or architecture diagram.

## Visual principles

1. A recognizable typographic silhouette before a detailed story.
2. Carbon and warm neutral fields instead of synthetic futurism.
3. One signal accent per composition when possible.
4. Reliable one-color and small-size reproduction.
5. Large working surfaces, disciplined grids, and visible metadata.
6. Real mechanisms and real work rather than “AI magic.”

## Identity architecture

**Hackers in the Loop** is the community umbrella. Individual projects retain their own names while carrying a shared endorsement:

- Threaded — a Hackers in the Loop project
- like-wasm — a Hackers in the Loop project
- Credential Gateway — stewarded by Hackers in the Loop
- fxclaw — built in the Hackers in the Loop ecosystem
- Kernel and hardware research — Hackers in the Loop open bench

The badge acts as a flag of stewardship and participation, not a corporate product badge.
