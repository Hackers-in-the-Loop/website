Book I · From Models to Custom Silicon

How Modern AI Models Work

This book gives a capable software engineer the mental models and practical skills needed to inspect, trace, compare, and explain modern AI model artifacts.

Every chapter can be read or watched as short lessons, and each way teaches the whole chapter. 2 of 23 chapters are published; the rest are coming.
