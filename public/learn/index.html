The series

From Models to Custom Silicon

A series of books for working engineers: how modern AI models work, how they are trained, how to run them fast, how their architectures differ, how to make them smaller and smarter, and how to build the hardware that runs them, from an FPGA to an ASIC.

Learn with us. Join the Hackers in the Loop Discord to discuss the books and lessons, ask questions, and learn more with other engineers.
